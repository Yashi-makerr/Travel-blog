Tirich Mir Expedition (7708m)

Hindu Kush Range, Chitral, Pakistan

Expedition Overview

Rising 7,708 meters (25,289 feet) above sea level, Tirich Mir is the highest mountain located in the Hindu Kush range and the highest peak outside the Karakoram and Himalayas. It stands as a majestic giant towering over the historic Chitral Valley in northern Pakistan.

First conquered in 1950 by a legendary Norwegian team led by philosopher and climber Arne Naess, the peak represents a true mountaineer's challenge. It is characterized by crevasse-ridden glaciers, steep ice walls, and unpredictable mountain weather, requiring sound acclimatization and robust physical endurance.

Our guided expedition package provides full logistics support, experienced high-altitude climbing guides, comfortable base camp dining, medical support, and all government-approved climbing clearances. Join us on this once-in-a-lifetime high-altitude adventure!

40-Day Expedition Schedule

A meticulous day-by-day plan built for acclimatization safety and optimal summit chances.

Day 1 Arrival in Islamabad international airport, transfer to hotel.
Day 2 Briefing at the Ministry of Tourism & official paperwork registration.
Day 3 Drive along the scenic Lowari Pass routes to Chitral town.
Day 4 Jeep drive through spectacular gorges to Shagrom village (Base Camp trek head).
Day 5 Begin trek. Hike through meadows and boulders to Shemlak campsite.
Day 6 Trek along the glacier moraine to Tirich Mir Base Camp (4,724m).
Day 7 - 34 Climbing window: Acclimatization rotations, load ferry to Camp 1 & 2, and summit attempts.
Day 35 - 40 Trek back to Shagrom, drive back to Islamabad, debriefing, and international flight home.
