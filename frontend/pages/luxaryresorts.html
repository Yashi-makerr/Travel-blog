The History of Luxury Hospitality

The concept of resorts dates back to the Roman Empire, where thermal baths and mineral springs in towns like Baiae functioned as the first health and relaxation retreats for elite citizens. During the 18th and 19th centuries in Europe, luxury spa towns like Bath, UK and Baden-Baden, Germany became major hubs of high society, drawing royalty and nobles who traveled for therapeutic waters and social gatherings.

In the late 19th century, the industrial revolution and expanding railroads paved the way for "Grand Hotels" along scenic coastlines and mountain ranges. Innovators like César Ritz redefined guest service, establishing new standards for private bathrooms, fine dining, and personalized hospitality. Today, luxury resorts have evolved to emphasize deep connection with nature and cultural immersion, offering private villas, infinity pools, and eco-luxury wilderness lodges designed to combine five-star service with local sustainability.

The 10 Most Exquisite Resorts on Earth

1. Sasaab Lodge, Kenya

Location: Samburu National Reserve, Kenya

Sasaab is a stunning luxury lodge situated in the wild savannah of northern Kenya. The design combines Moroccan and Swahili architectures, featuring expansive open-air thatched villas with private plunge pools overlooking the Ewaso Nyiro River, where elephants gather daily to bathe. Guests can experience game drives, camel treks, and fly camping under the stars.

2. Omni Amelia Island Resort, Florida

Location: Amelia Island, Florida, USA

This premium oceanfront resort is nestled on 1,350 acres of live oak forests and pristine sand dunes. Offering luxury rooms, all with direct Atlantic Ocean views, the property features a massive pool deck, championship golf courses, tennis courts, and a full-service spa. It provides the perfect blend of coastal relaxation and family activities.

3. Playa Largo Resort & Spa, Florida

Location: Key Largo, Florida, USA

Set along the tranquil Florida Bay, Playa Largo offers a private beach, dock, water sports, and beachside hammocks. Rooms are split across towers, private bungalows, and a beach house with its own pool. Sol by the Sea restaurant serves fresh, local seafood right on the shoreline, capturing the laidback spirit of the Florida Keys.

4. Silverado Resort and Spa, Napa Valley

Location: Napa, California, USA

Silverado is a historic Napa Valley estate surrounded by vineyards, featuring two PGA championship golf courses, tennis courts, and a grand spa. Guests can stay in private condo-style suites and enjoy farm-to-table dining, local wine tastings, and cycling routes winding through Northern California's famous wine region.

5. Sirikoi Lodge, Kenya

Location: Lewa Wildlife Conservancy, Kenya

Sirikoi is an eco-luxury safari lodge offering high-end comfort in a wild environment. Located in the Lewa Wildlife Conservancy, a UNESCO World Heritage site home to endangered rhinos, the lodge features hand-crafted tents and private cottages surrounded by lush gardens, offering close encounters with grazing giraffes and zebras.

6. The Romanos, Costa Navarino

Location: Costa Navarino, Greece

Located in the historic Messinia region of Greece, The Romanos is a luxury beachfront resort overlooking the sapphire Ionian Sea. Featuring elegant marble suites, private plunge pools, and low-slung villas wrapped around infinity lagoons, the property offers philosophy walks in ancient olive groves and organic wine harvesting.

7. Zemi Beach House, Anguilla

Location: Shoal Bay East, Anguilla

Zemi Beach House is a boutique resort situated on six acres along the white sands of Shoal Bay East. It features a unique spa set inside a 300-year-old Thai rice barn imported to the island, offering a volcanic mud bath and hammam treatments. The contemporary rooms are complete with personalized Caribbean minibars.

8. Le Barthélemy Hotel & Spa, St. Barts

Location: Grand Cul-de-Sac, St. Barts

Perfectly situated on a crescent bay along the island's northeastern shore, Le Barthélemy is an understated, elegant resort. Offering enormous rooms with wood details and private plunge pools, it faces a shallow, turtle-filled lagoon. The gourmet dining at Amis St. Barth serves modern French-Caribbean fusion cuisine.

9. Hôtel Barrière Le Carl Gustaf, St. Barts

Location: Gustavia, St. Barts

Perched high on a hill overlooking the harbor of Gustavia, this iconic hotel has been reimagined by designers Gilles & Boissier. It features cream stone, linens, and private bungalows wrapped in tropical greenery. Guests have direct access to Shell Beach and can dine on French-Mediterranean fare by Michelin-starred chef Pierre Gagnaire.

10. Laucala Island Resort, Fiji

Location: Laucala Island, Fiji

Laucala Island is a private island resort located in the northern archipelago of Fiji. Spanning 3,500 acres of coconut groves, sandy beaches, and rainforest-covered volcanic peaks, the resort features 25 private villas designed after traditional Fijian bures, complete with private pools, and access to a submarine.
